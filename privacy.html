Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Content Agency is a private tool used by one owner to make short educational videos and upload them to the owner's own YouTube channels (Mysticore, Gospel TV and Money Lecture). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • Upload videos (scope youtube.upload) to the owner's own channels, with a title and description the owner approved.
  • Read public information (scope youtube.readonly): the owner's own videos' view, like and comment counts, and publicly listed popular Shorts, to decide which topics to make next.

The app does not read private messages, subscriber lists, comments' authors or any viewer's personal data, and it does not access anyone else's account.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only in the owner's private settings: on the owner's computer and in the owner's private cloud workspace. Video statistics are stored in the app's private database. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and nothing is used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored statistics are deleted on request.

Google policies

The app's use of YouTube follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the channel owner, through the contact details on the Mysticore, Gospel TV or Money Lecture YouTube channels.
